Ratios are candidate / baseline; speedup above 1 favours the candidate using the metric’s declared direction. Ratios require declared semantic qualification, equal contracts and metric scopes, and complete matching rounds. Baseline selection filters precomputed comparisons; the browser does not calculate statistics. Metric and baseline filters leave unsupported and censored work visible.
