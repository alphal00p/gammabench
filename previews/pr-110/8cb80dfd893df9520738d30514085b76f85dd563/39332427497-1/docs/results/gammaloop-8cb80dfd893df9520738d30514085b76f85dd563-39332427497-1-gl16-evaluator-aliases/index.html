    How to read these results

    • Observed ranges describe the captured samples, not confidence intervals.
    • Profile scopes may cover more work than completion metrics; inclusive costs are not additive phases.

    Summary medians use completed measurement samples. Warmups are excluded. Timeouts and missing samples remain visible and suppress pair rankings. Ratios are candidate / baseline; speedup above 1 favours the candidate using the metric’s declared direction. Ratios require declared semantic qualification, equal contracts and metric scopes, and complete matching rounds. Baseline selection filters precomputed comparisons; the browser does not calculate statistics. Metric and baseline filters leave unsupported and censored work visible.
